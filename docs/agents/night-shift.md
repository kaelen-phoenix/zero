# Night shift

What the factory does while idle: scout, research, unjam, and the reverts of what it integrated on its own.

- `src/line/scout.ts`: the night shift. While the factory is idle (no queued or running jobs, andon off, budget left, inside the optional `nightShift.hours`), the daemon runs `scoutPass` in the background: the read-only `scout` station (`agents/scout.md`) looks at each line once per period (the day, or the window) and zero keeps only the proposals that pass `filterProposals` (no protected paths, no new credentials, no repeated titles, at most 3). On per-use billing the scout spends at most `limits.usdPerNight` per period. Proposals live in the `proposals` table of `state.db`. `approveProposal`/`rejectProposal` in `app.ts` decide them; approving opens a normal job with source `{kind: 'night-shift'}`. Each stored proposal gets `proposalAutonomy` (the line's `lineAutonomy`, restricted by `research.autonomy` for every proposal with a `researchNote`, whatever scout's `thirdParty` flag or its paths say; `proposalRequest` in `app.ts` states the note in the job's request as web data, not instructions). Under `build`/`auto`, and below `nightShift.maxAutoJobsPerDay` across lines (`autonomousJobsSince`), scout approves it itself: a job with source `{kind: 'night-shift', proposal, autonomy}` and the proposal's `decidedBy: 'night-shift'`. The runner parks `build` jobs (and `auto` research-born jobs whose diff touches a manifest, unless research is `auto`) at `before-integrate` with onTimeout `reject` (`nightShiftHold`); `approveProposal` then answers that job and `rejectProposal` stops it. The daemon considers owner jobs first (`ownerFirst`) and starts an autonomous job only when nothing else is queued or running. Rejections go to the line's townhall with the owner's reason, and scout's prompt lists the line's last 20 rejected or reverted proposals (`recentRejected`). Per line, `scoutIssues` lists through `IssueApi.unlabeled` the open unlabeled issues written by writers (TRUSTED) not yet linked by a proposal (at most 20, newest first) as data in scout's prompt; a proposal's `issue` is kept as `sourceIssue`/`sourceIssueUrl` only when it was listed. At the start of each `scoutPass` (after the off/hours and andon checks), `promotePending` takes on pending proposals of lines now at `build`/`auto` through `takeOnItsOwn` (oldest first, within `maxAutoJobsPerDay`), never third-party ones (`thirdParty` or `touchesManifest`). `ownerOnly` keeps a proposal at `propose` whatever the autonomy: born from a business note (`business`), flagged `commercial` by scout (license, pricing, charges, payments, legal text) or with paths matched by `touchesLegal` in `policy.ts`; `nightShiftHold` also holds any autonomous job whose changed files match `touchesLegal`. The briefing and the panel's Investigación tab split notes into "Negocio" and "Técnica".
- `src/line/reverts.ts`: `detectReverts` (a daemon pass on the intake interval) marks `reverted` the night-shift proposals whose job integrated on its own (no `approvals.integrate`; delivery mode `merge` with a commit, or `pull-request` with the merge or squash commit that `automerge.ts` or the pass itself records from GitHub's `mergeCommitSha`) once the base branch (local, or `<remote>/<base>` after a fetch on pull-request lines) has a `This reverts commit <sha>` commit or, for pull requests, GitHub's `Reverts owner/repo#N`, and posts a `propuesta revertida` lesson to the line's townhall. A pull request GitHub reports closed without merging is asked about again only 24 hours later (`reverts:closed:<job>` in kv, an ISO date), since it can be reopened and merged; the date goes when it is seen open or merged with a commit, and a store failure only means asking as usual. Errors from GitHub open no wait.
- `src/line/research.ts`: the research station (`agents/research.md`, access `web`: on Claude the read tools plus `WebSearch`, elsewhere like `read`). While the factory is idle the daemon runs `researchPass` with the night shift's gates; only lines with a line-level research skill (`lines[].stations.research.skills`) research. Each (line, skill) pair runs once per local day (kv `research:<line>:<skill>`), with a prompt naming that single skill and at most `research.perLinePerDay` notes of its own, least recently researched first. Skills listed in `lines[].stations.research.business` (`businessSkills` in config.ts; names not in `skills` are ignored) write business notes (`skill`, `business` on the note). Notes (http(s) sources only, local paths scrubbed by `sanitizeNotes`) live in the `research_notes` table; scout's prompt gets its line's notes of the last 7 days as data. `scoutSpent` counts what scout, research and the packages' stations were really charged (`billedUsd` in `budget.ts`, like `jobBilledUsd`: subscription calls do not count, unknown billing does), since they share `limits.usdPerNight`; the daemon runs them one after the other in a single shared idle run (`idlePasses`: scout, then research, then the packages' stations), so each pass computes its cap after the other's spend is recorded. `requestFromResearchNote` in `app.ts` opens a job with source `{kind: 'research'}`. `research.autonomy` caps every research-born proposal (see `proposalAutonomy` in scout.ts); scout's output carries `researchNote` (kept only when it is one of the line's notes in the prompt) and `thirdParty`.
- Research on demand, "express" (`src/line/research.ts`, asked for with `requestInvestigation(ctx, topic, line?, now)` in `app.ts`): at most one pending `ResearchRequest` in the whole factory (kv `research-demand`: `requestedAt`, `line`, `topic`; the topic trimmed and passed through `scrubLocal`, the line the only one without `package` when none is given), refused without writing anything when one is pending, with the andon on, the budget reached, `research: off`, an unknown, package or ambiguous line (`researchOnDemandProblem`), or a topic blank or over `RESEARCH_TOPIC_CHARS` (2000). `researchOnDemandPass` makes one call on that line with `researchTopicPrompt` (the topic, the line's researched topics, the web rules, no skill), whatever the jobs, the hours, `nightShift`, `perLinePerDay` or the line's skills; it neither reads nor writes `research:<line>:<skill>`, so the regular runs keep their turn. `researchOnDemandReady` leaves it pending while the andon is on, the budget is reached (`researchBudgetReached`: `budgetState`, or the day's dollars spent on an engine not billed by subscription) or research has no engine. The call's `maxBudgetUsd` is the smaller of `research.usdPerRun` (default 2) and what is left of `limits.usdPerDay`, with nothing to cap on a subscription; its span carries `zero.on_demand` (`ON_DEMAND_ATTR`, through `AgentTrace.attrs`), which `scoutSpent` skips, so it counts in the day's budget and never in `limits.usdPerNight`. At most `NOTES_PER_RUN` (5) notes, through `sanitizeNotes`, without `skill` and never business. The request is cleared right before the run, whose `ResearchRunRecord` (kv `research-demand-runs`, the last `RUNS_KEPT` (10), newest first, `readResearchRuns`) is written at the start and at the end: `asked`, `line`, `topic`, `startedAt`, `endedAt`, `outcome` (`ok`, with a reason when nothing with sources was found; `failed`; `stopped` by the andon, a shutdown or the cap, which store no note; `not-run` when the line went away or research was turned off first), `reason` and the ids in `notes`. The topic never goes to the log. `closeInterruptedResearch` ends as `stopped` a run a crashed daemon left open. `waitResearchStart` and `waitResearchEnd` wait for the record of a `requestedAt` to start or to end (by default the station's timeout plus a minute). It is asked for with `zero investigar "<tema>" [--line] [--esperar]` (starts the factory, waits up to 2 minutes for the start, exits 1 on a refusal; `--esperar` then waits for the end and prints the notes) or the chat's `zero_investigate` (the same wait as `zero_unjam`; over the remote chat it needs `zero:act` and is not held for confirmation). `FactoryStatus.research` (`ResearchOnDemand`: `enabled`, the `lines` it may be asked for, `pending`, `runs`) is shown by `formatStatus` with `researchPendingText` and `describeResearchRun` in `src/format.ts` (the topic in one line of at most 120 characters), and the briefing's `## Corridas a demanda` lists the pending request and the runs that started or ended in the period. The MCP server's `withFactory` adds `researchNotice` of every ended run no chat told yet to a successful answer, as a content block of its own, only in chats that may act (`canAct`); `takeUntoldResearch` marks them in kv `research-demand-told` (their `asked`) in one write transaction, and a failure there is only logged.
- `src/line/unjam.ts`: the unjam station (`agents/unjam.md`, access `read`, cwd `line.repo`). `unjamPass` runs in the daemon's shared idle run after scout and before research, with the night shift's gates (`unjam` not `off`, hours, andon, no queued or running job, budget, an engine for `unjam`, the rest of `limits.usdPerNight` as each call's cap; `unjam` is in `IDLE_STATIONS`, so `scoutSpent` counts it), once per line per period (kv `unjam-pass:<line>`) and never for a line without jams. Before collecting jams, it and `urgentUnjamPass` run `discardResolved` (`src/line/discard.ts`, see [Production line](production-line.md)), so a failed job whose work is already in the base branch is closed and never diagnosed. `collectJams` gathers a line's jams from its records alone (failed jobs of the last 7 days with their feedback, waiting jobs, blocked auto-merge pull requests with `conflict` from `backoff.mergeable: false`, the repository's `checks-outage:`/`checks-billing:` records, intake problems, pending cleanups, a box that is not ready with `isolation: container` as a single machine jam on the first configured line without `package` (none with `isolation: worktree`), and an `install` jam on the line whose GitHub repository is `install:freshness`'s `repository` while `installBehind`, whose text says whether `selfUpdate.enabled` is on or off and, when on, the last self-update entry's outcome), each with an id stable within the pass; `withCheckLogs` adds the end (`LOG_CHARS`) of a failed Actions job's log through the optional `MergeApi.failedLog`, once per repository and check name (GitHub pass `unjam` of the meter; any failure leaves the jam without log). `acceptCauses` keeps causes naming jams of the prompt (at most `CAUSES_PER_LINE`, scrubbed) and `unjamLine` stores them in kv `unjam:<line>` (`Diagnosis`, `readDiagnosis`), each with `seen` (`countSeen`: the earlier diagnosis's same-title cause plus one), then publishes a `traba repetida: <title>` townhall lesson for each cause with 2 jams or `seen` 2 or more (`publishLessons`, a failure only logged). Every unjam proposal's `why` is the diagnosis plus `evidenceText`, computed by zero from the cause's jams (count, jobs, pull request urls, `seen`). `openCapabilities` turns each `capability` cause (target `line` from the lines listed in the prompt) into one unjam proposal on the target line carrying `forJobs` (the `for_jobs` column: the cause's failed jobs whose reason starts with `SPEC_REFUSAL` in `runner.ts`, without a spec, not carried by another pending or approved unjam proposal nor resent), through `takeOnItsOwn` with the target line's autonomy and the same title dedupe; an unknown or package line, or no job refused at spec, makes the cause `none` with a `todo`. `resendAfterCapability` (the daemon's `resendRefused`, on the intake interval) resends each still `failed` job of an approved one once (kv `unjam:resent:<job>` = the new job) through `requestWork` with its request, line and source, after the proposal's job entered (delivered `merge` with a commit, or MergeState `merged`) and, when the target line's repository is `install:freshness`'s `repository`, only with a record checked after that job finished without `problem`, `behind` or `staleBuild`; each resend is a `capacidad agregada` townhall lesson. `openFixes` opens one proposal with `origin: 'unjam'` per `fix` cause (no repeat of a title, by `normalizeTitle`, held by a pending, rejected, reverted or unfinished approved unjam proposal of the line, or by an approved one whose job was refused at spec, `refusedAtSpec`, less than `REFUSED_HOLD_DAYS` (7) days after it finished (`finishedAt`, else `updatedAt`), while any other final job releases it at once; `holdsTitle` at the pass's `now`, shared with `openCapabilities`; nothing for protected paths or new credentials) through `takeOnItsOwn`, whose jobs get source `{kind: 'night-shift', proposal, autonomy, origin: 'unjam'}` (priority `baja`) within `unjam.maxJobsPerDay` (`unjamJobsLeft`, counting retries) and `nightShift.maxAutoJobsPerDay`; `requestBlock` presents them as drafted by the unjam station from the factory's records. `retryCauses` calls `retryJob` in `app.ts` (behind `zero reintentar <trabajo>`: a failed job with a spec back to `queued` from its checkpoint with `attempt` 0, a failed job without a spec whose reason does not start with `SPEC_REFUSAL` back from the start, or a delivered auto-merge pull request in conflict or after its checks fixes with `attempt` 0 and its MergeState without `resolving`/`resolutions`/`conflict`/`fixing`/`fixes`/`backoff`, so `takeBack` or `fixChecks` takes it; a job refused at spec is still refused) once per job's life, within `unjamJobsLeft`, (kv `unjam:retried:<job>`) on `build`/`auto` lines; on `propose` the cause's `todo` names the command. `urgentUnjamPass` (the daemon's background pass on the intake interval, outside `idlePasses` and the night shift's hours, off in `once` runs unless `DaemonOptions.urgentUnjam` is injected) diagnoses a line during the day when one of its pull requests has been blocked (`MergeState.blockedSince`) at least `unjam.urgentAfterHours` (default 2, 0 turns it off), with the andon off, budget left, an engine for `unjam`, a free slot under `concurrency`, no queued job that is not `lowPriority`, at most once per line per that many hours (kv `unjam-urgent:<line>`) and the rest of `limits.usdPerNight` as its cap. A `train` cause makes `trainCauses` start the line's train with the cause's pull requests on `build`/`auto` lines (none when the line has one), and on `propose` lines adds `zero tren <línea> <números>` to its `todo`. Each cause may carry `who` (`CAUSE_WHO`: `owner`, `author`, `github`, `factory`), which `acceptCauses` keeps and sets to `owner` for a `none` cause without one. `jamViews` in `app.ts` (a `machine` cause whose title an earlier line already shows is left out, so one machine problem counts once; and so is one whose jams are all `job:`/`pr:` ids of jobs now stopped, or delivered with no blocked merge, `closedCause`) gives `FactoryStatus.jams` (with `who`, `owner` when a cause with a `todo` has none), shown by `jamLine` in `src/format.ts` (`JAM_KIND_TEXT`, `missingText`: `qué falta: <todo> · lo tiene que hacer: vos | quien abrió el pull request | GitHub | la fábrica`) as the `Trabas:` block of `formatStatus` and the panel's summary, the briefing's `## Trabas` and, for causes with action `none`, the monthly report's "Trabas" section (`LineReport.jams`).
- The unjam station on demand (`src/line/unjam.ts`, asked for with `requestUnjam(ctx, line?, now)` in `app.ts`): at most one pending `UnjamRequest` in the whole factory (kv `unjam-demand`, with no line for every line that is not a package's), refused without writing anything when one is pending, with the andon on, the budget reached, `unjam: off` or an unknown or package line (`onDemandProblem`). `unjamOnDemandPass` runs it through `unjamPass` with its `demand` argument (`UnjamDemand`): the same `unjamLine`, caps, dedupe, retries, autonomy and `limits.usdPerNight` cap, but outside the night shift's hours, with it off, with jobs queued or running and even for a line already diagnosed; it neither reads nor writes `unjam-pass:<line>` nor `unjam-urgent:<line>`, so the regular passes keep their turn. `unjamOnDemandReady` leaves it pending while the andon is on or the budget is reached. The request is cleared right before the run, whose `UnjamRunRecord` (kv `unjam-demand-last`, `readUnjamRun`; both keys stay outside the `unjam:` prefix, where a line's diagnosis is) is written at the start and at the end: `asked`, `startedAt`, `endedAt`, `outcome` (`ok`, `failed` naming the lines without a valid diagnosis, `stopped` with the reason `unjamPass` returns when the andon, the budget, the night cap, a missing engine or a shutdown cuts it, `not-run` when the line went away or the station was turned off first), `reason`, the `jams` and `causes` of each line looked at, and `closed` (optional, absent when none): the `{ line, job }` of every failed job the pass's `discardResolved` closed, from every line even when the request names one, reported through `UnjamDemand.closed` (its `onClosed`) and rewritten into the record on each closure, so a cut run, a throwing `unjamPass` or a crash keeps them. `closeInterruptedUnjam` ends as `stopped` the one a crashed daemon left open. `waitUnjamStart` waits for the record of a `requestedAt` to start. Jams are collected only once the run started, after whatever `unjamPass` does ahead of its lines (`discardResolved`, which closes the jobs already resolved), so a job closed by then is no jam. The owner asks for it with `zero destrabar [línea]` (exit 1 with the reason on a refusal; otherwise it starts the factory and waits `STATION_WAIT_MS`), the chat's `zero_unjam` (the same wait as `zero_package_run`; over the remote chat it needs `zero:act` and is not held for confirmation) or the panel's "Destrabar ahora" button (see [Panel](panel.md)). `FactoryStatus.unjam` (`UnjamOnDemand`: `enabled`, the `lines` it may be asked for, `pending`, `last`) is shown by `formatStatus` with `unjamPendingText` and `describeUnjamRun` in `src/format.ts` (which, with closures, adds after the status segment `cerró N trabajos que ya estaban resueltos (<línea>: <id>, …; …)`, grouped by line, at most `UNJAM_CLOSED_SHOWN` (5) ids and `, y M más`; a missing or non-array `closed` adds nothing), and the briefing's `## Corridas a demanda` lists the pending request and the last run when it started or ended in the period.
