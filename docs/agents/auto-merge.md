# Auto-merge

Pull requests after delivery: automatic and requested merges, conflicts, failed checks, outages, billing, cancelled runs and trains.

- `src/line/automerge.ts`: merges pull requests of `autoMerge` lines once GitHub reports them ready (polled by the daemon); a blocked one keeps `backoff` in its `MergeState` and, with the same head and `mergeable`, is asked only for `pullRequest` until the wait (5 minutes doubling up to 60) ends. A pull request with `mergeable: false` (or a head whose `updateBranch` answered a merge conflict, `MergeState.conflict`) goes back to the queue through `takeBack` when the job has a spec, the pull request used fewer than `MAX_RESOLUTIONS` (2) conflict resolutions in its life (`MergeState.resolutions`, not the build's attempts; the job goes back with `attempt` at most `maxAttempts - 1`), its local branch is at the head, that head was not tried (`MergeState.resolving`, kept by every write) and the base fetches; otherwise it is told as blocked with `en conflicto con la rama base` and, when the resolutions are used up or that head's resolution failed, what is missing. `mergeDecision` decides the conflict before any check, and a head in conflict gets no `checks`, `behind`, `updateBranch`, rerun, outage or billing handling. A head with failed checks that GitHub does not call mergeable is tested locally first (`conflictsLocally`: fetch of the base, `git merge-tree --write-tree` in the line's repository); a conflict found there is recorded as `MergeState.conflict` and goes through `takeBack` before any check handling. When failed checks (no billing refusal, no outage, every check of the tip ended) have nothing left in `unblock` (base not ahead or `MAX_REFRESHES` used, runs already rerun at this tip), `fixChecks` puts the job back in the queue to fix them on its own branch (checkpoint `spec`, station `build`, `attempt` 0, `approvals.integrate` dropped), with `checksFeedback` as its feedback (each failed check's name, the tests `failedTests` finds in its `failedLog` and the end of that log, `FIX_LOG_CHARS`, through `scrubLocal`, framed as CI data) and the prRefreshKey mark with `checks` (that feedback, already `started`): `runJob` merges no base, `buildLoop` keeps the checks in the prompt and fails an attempt that leaves the branch at the head, `updateProblem` only asks for the earlier head, and the push goes to the same pull request; a failed fix ends `delivered` like a failed resolution. Its own limit is `MergeState.fixes` (at most `MAX_CHECK_FIXES`, 2) and each tip is tried once (`MergeState.fixing`), both kept by every save; a busy repository makes it wait its turn, and a used-up limit or a failed tip is told with `fallaron los checks …` and `zero reintentar <job>`. A job without a spec, a branch not at the head, a pending cleanup or a base that does not fetch is left as it is. The kv mark `prRefreshKey` (`src/line/finish.ts`) drives the update: `runJob` runs `WorkCopy.mergeBase` on the base commit it pins in the mark (`baseCommit`, since `origin/<base>` is shared and moves during the job; a clean merge resumes at verify with no attempt spent; a conflict goes to build as an attempt, `buildLoop` restarting the merge from the head before every engine call and refusing what `conflictMarkers` finds or a branch without the base), checks again before integrating, and `integrate` with `existing` compares `ls-remote` with the head and pushes without force, opening nothing. With the mark, `finalizeJob` turns `failed` or `stopped` into `delivered` with the original delivery, moves the branch back to the head and posts a `blocker`. `adoptPullRequest` in `app.ts` takes an open pull request of the line's GitHub repository that zero did not open (same repository, not a fork, to the line's `baseBranch`, its branch used by no unfinished or delivered job, not checked out, no local branch elsewhere): it fetches the branch as a local branch at the head, leaves the local backup `respaldo/pr<N>` (or `respaldo/pr<N>-<job>` when that one is at another commit), and queues a job with source `{kind: 'pull-request'}`, a spec written by zero, that branch, base `<remote>/<base>`, the pull request as its delivery and the `prRefreshKey` mark `{ head }`, so `runJob` updates it like its own; `conflictFeedback` names the `openspec/changes/<name>/` folders the branch adds or changes, and `requestBlock` frames the job as a third party's pull request. `adoptPullRequests` (behind `zero actualizar-pr <línea> <número>...`, printed by `formatAdoptions`) adopts several in the order given, telling each refusal without stopping the rest, and the command exits 1 when any was refused. `mergeRequested` in `automerge.ts` (behind `mergePullRequest` in `app.ts`, `zero fusionar <trabajo | url>` and the chat's `zero_merge`, with an injectable `MergeApiFactory`; the real one is the line's signed `githubMerges` outside the meter) merges a delivered pull request on request, with or without `autoMerge`, only when `mergeDecision` says `merge`; otherwise it returns `merged: false` with why and writes nothing to GitHub, and a refused merge call comes back the same way. A merge records a `zero.merge` span (`zero.merge.via`, `zero.merge.commit`, `zero.merge.url`), MergeState `merged` (so `watchedJobs` drops it) and a `fundido a pedido` townhall lesson, all best effort after the merge.
- Failed checks, outages and billing (`src/line/automerge.ts`): Before leaving a pull request blocked by failed checks, `unblock` in `automerge.ts` tries what needs no code change, through the optional `MergeApi.behind`/`updateBranch`/`rerun`: behind its base, GitHub merges the base in (`MergeState.refreshes`, at most `MAX_REFRESHES` in its life; `refreshing` holds the repository so its pull requests are refreshed one at a time, for at most 2 hours, and the others wait blocked with `espera su turno`, looked at every 5 minutes), then `followRemoteTip` fetches the new tip into the line's local branch; up to date, the failed GitHub Actions runs (`Check.run`, from `actionsRun`) are run again once per tip (`MergeState.rerun`), which holds the repository the same way (`refreshing.rerun`, until every check of the tip ended) and waits its turn the same way. Nothing is started for a pull request with a failed check while another of its checks still runs (it waits with `esperando los demás checks`, and a hold is only released once every check of the tip ended), nor while another watched pull request of the repository delivered in the last 2 hours is still `waiting` for its first checks (`firstChecksElsewhere`, read from kv): CI that shares an environment keeps one run waiting and cancels the rest. A GitHub that refuses changes nothing, and limits are rethrown. A shared environment that is down is told from a change (`ChecksOutage`, kv `checksOutageKey`): the failed checks of each tip are remembered for 24 hours (kv `checksFailuresKey`, with `Check.at`, the time GitHub says it ended; failures without one are never compared in time), and the same check failed in two watched pull requests of the repository at most `OUTAGE_WINDOW_MS` apart, or also failed at the tip of the base branch (read with `checks(base)` right before an update or a rerun, a base that cannot be read saying nothing), records the outage. While it lasts a pull request failing one of its checks is left `blocked` with `outageWhy`, marked `MergeState.outage`, without comment, update or rerun. After the pull requests, `followOutage` (end of `pollMerges`) forgets an outage no marked pull request is left for, ends it when one of its checks ended well after its `lastFailure` at a watched tip or at the base (read at most every 5 minutes, `baseLookAt`), recording kv `checksRecoveredKey` so failures that ended before then count for nothing (neither between pull requests nor at the base), and clears the marked pull requests' `backoff`; otherwise, from `nextProbeAt` (30 minutes after the last failure, doubling per failed probe up to 240) and with the repository free, it probes by running the failed runs of the oldest marked pull request again (`ChecksOutage.probe`, `refreshing` with `rerun` so `holds` keeps the repository, its own `MergeState.rerun` untouched). Once it is over, each marked pull request gets one more rerun even with `rerun` at its tip, one at a time, which clears its mark. `factoryStatus` reports each outage under way as `outages` (kv `checks-outage:<repo>`, with the pull requests whose `MergeState.outage` is its `since`) and leaves those out of `merges`, so `formatStatus`, the panel's summary and the briefing show one `Entorno caído en <repo> desde HH:MM (checks: …)` line per outage (`outageText` in `automerge.ts`, shared with `outageWhy`; `outageLine` in `src/format.ts`) instead of one per pull request. A failed check run GitHub never started over the account's billing (`billingRefusal` on its output or, when it has them, its annotations, marking `Check.billing`; `Check.started` is the run's `started_at` once in progress or completed) holds its pull request before any outage logic: no comment, refresh, rerun or failure record, `MergeState.billing` (kept by every save, like `billingRerun`), `blocked` with `billingText` and a backoff, and one kv `checks-billing:<repo>` record per repository (`ChecksBilling`: `since`, `last`, `jobs`) that `factoryStatus` lists as `billing` (its pull requests left out of `merges`) and `formatStatus` (`billingLine`), the panel's summary and the briefing show once. After the outages, `followBilling` (end of `pollMerges`) waits for a non-billing check `started` after the record's `last` at a watched tip or at the base (read at most every 5 minutes), records it as `recovered` (a later refusal clears it), then with the repository free reruns the refused runs of the oldest held pull request, one per pass and each run once in its life (`billingRerun`), clearing its mark and holding the repository with `refreshing.rerun`; a pull request refused again on runs already rerun is only told, and the record goes once it holds none.
- Cancelled runs, the train and simple conflicts (in `src/line/`). A check whose conclusion is `cancelled` (a newer run of the same concurrency group cancelled it) is `Check.cancelled`, never a failure: `mergeDecision` gives `cancelled` when every check ended and only cancelled ones did not pass, and `rerunCancelled` in `automerge.ts` runs those runs again (oldest delivered first, one pull request per repository at a time through `refreshing.rerun`, without touching `MergeState.rerun`), at most `MAX_CANCELLED_RERUNS` (3) per tip (`MergeState.cancelledReruns`), then blocks saying the runs keep getting cancelled. No pull request of a repository is merged while another watched one was last seen with a check neither done nor started (`queuedElsewhere`, waiting with a reason naming it). `MergeState.blockedSince` keeps the first blocked time while it stays blocked (`stampBlocked`). `src/line/conflicts.ts` (`resolveSimply`, `resolveDiff3`): when merging the base conflicts, `runJob` first resolves every conflicted file without a model (equal sides, sides that only touch single-line imports, kept as their union with ours first, and both sides adding lines where the base had none, ours then theirs); only when every hunk of every file resolves is the merge committed and the job resumes at verify with no attempt spent, otherwise the conflict goes to build as before. `src/line/train.ts`: a line's integration train (kv `train:<line>`, `Train`: `entries` in merge order, each with `job`, `url` and, for a pull request the factory did not open, the verified `head`, and an optional `block` with `pull`, `what`, `who` and `author`). `watchedJobs` watches train pull requests even without `autoMerge`; only the head is acted on (merged only when `mergeDecision` says `merge`, updated, rerun or taken back) and the others wait as `MergeState.train`; `advanceTrains` drops merged or gone heads and gives the new head back once its `resolutions`, `refreshes`, `resolving` and `conflict` (`MergeState.trainHead`). An adopted head is merged only at the head its job verified (`pinTrainHead`, called by the runner after verify, the reviews and a successful integration, also kept as kv `verified-head:<job>` for later trains; nothing is pinned at adoption), and GitHub's `updateBranch` is never used on it: behind its base with failed checks, `takeBack` with `refresh` (counted in `refreshes`) sends its job to merge the base, verify and push, which pins the new head; an adopted head with none (its job gave up or was stopped) stops the train with `UNVERIFIED` and `who: owner`, and another head on GitHub stops it with `PUSHED` and `who: author`. `startTrain` in `app.ts` (behind `zero tren <línea> [<número>...]`) orders the line's delivered unmerged pull requests oldest delivery first, or the numbers given in that order, adopting through `adoptPullRequest` each one the factory did not deliver, and refuses an unknown line, a line not integrating by pull request or one with a train before writing anything. `trainViews` gives `FactoryStatus.trains`, shown by `trainLine` in `src/format.ts` as `Tren de <línea>:` in `formatStatus`, the panel summary and the briefing's `## Trenes de integración`, with where it stopped through `missingText` (`qué falta: … · lo tiene que hacer: …`, `whoText`). `mergePullRequest` also merges, pinned to the head it read and writing no job, an open green pull request of a line's GitHub repository that the factory did not deliver.
