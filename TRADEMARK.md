# Trademark policy

This policy explains what you can do with the name and logos of the zero in the loop project, and what needs permission first. Anyone can use and build on the code, and nobody should be led to believe that something comes from the project when it does not.

## The marks

This policy covers "the marks":

- the name "zero in the loop";
- the project's logos, including the logo files in [`site/brand/`](site/brand/).

## The code's license

The code is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The license and the marks are separate things: section 6 of the license grants no permission to use the marks, beyond what is reasonable and customary to describe where the work comes from.

This policy does not change the license of the code, and it does not restrict any right the license gives you over the code. It is only about the marks.

## Uses that do not need permission

- **Forks.** Fork and modify the code, publicly or privately. If you distribute a modified version, see the next section.
- **Describing.** Name the project truthfully to talk about it, to say where your work comes from or to state compatibility, for example "works with zero in the loop" or "based on zero in the loop".
- **Unmodified copies.** Redistribute unmodified copies under the name.
- **Technical identifiers.** Keep the `zero` command, file names, configuration keys and similar identifiers when compatibility needs them.
- **The signature.** Keep the "built with zero in the loop" signature that zero adds to what it builds. It says which tool was used; it does not mean that the project endorses the result.

None of these uses may suggest that the project made, sponsors or approves your work.

## Uses that need permission

Ask for prior written permission to:

- **Products.** Use "zero in the loop", or a name similar enough to cause confusion, as the name or branding of a derived product or a fork.
- **Services.** Use it as the name or branding of a service, including hosted or managed services and training.
- **Logos.** Use the logos, or a variation of them, as the branding of a product, fork or service.
- **Official status.** Suggest an official edition, an endorsement or an affiliation, for example "official zero in the loop Enterprise Edition".

If you distribute a modified version without that permission, use another name and remove the logos. You can still say that it is based on zero in the loop.

## The word "zero"

"zero" is a generic word. The project claims no exclusivity over the word "zero" on its own; this policy is about the full name "zero in the loop" and the logos.

## How to ask for permission

Write through the contact section of the site, <https://zerointheloop.com/#contact>, and say what you want to name or show, where and for what. If you are not sure whether a use needs permission, ask there too.

This policy is a plain explanation of how the project wants its marks to be used. It is not legal advice.
